# Engineering catalogue — original AutoWoW concepts

**Quantified materials:** [Engineering shopping lists](MATERIAL_BILLS.md#engineering). Costs are proposed; the table states output quantities and marks unimplemented relic cores.

Status: design only. This is a proposed engineering catalogue, not a native item, recipe, spell, SQL, client, or runtime catalogue. It contains exactly 18 original concepts: 8 field, 4 masterwork, and 6 legendary.

The catalogue emphasizes weak-weapon replacement and practical core gear. It deliberately uses only a few consumable or deployable forms; these are not twenty interchangeable trinkets. A commissioned result keeps a versioned blueprint, item identity, maker lineage, stat allocation, signature family, and support finish. The tier is an item and role budget, not an uncapped boss legacy.

## Bot selection and compatibility

Bots select from actual demand, missing core gear, available stock, learned engineering technique, station access, and the buyer's role. The planner first checks the accepted equipment form and class, then ammunition or payload route, then stat budget and signature vocabulary. A bot can commission a result from an engineer without being an engineer; the delivered item must be an admitted normal equipment form that the buyer can actually equip. Commissioning does not rewrite profession or class restrictions.

Fieldbots use compatible ordinary equipment when a normal route exists. They refuse a device that requires an unavailable profession, an unadmitted client form, an unavailable ammunition or consumable route, or a native target/effect route that has not been qualified. An engineer-only part may be installed by a qualified engineer but cannot silently turn an incompatible weapon, head, or armor slot into a usable item.

Field ammunition, cartridges, repair coils, and service supplies consume real stocked inputs and use only qualified native weapon, inventory, and maintenance routes. There are no infinite free charges, ambient triggers, or client-only effects. Masterwork and legendary deployables require legal placement and native lifecycle handling; controlled explosions require native hostile-target, range, cooldown, friendly-protection, and client validation. No catalogue entry summons a forced arrival or an invulnerable bodyguard. A support finish follows the equipped bearer and eligible group/range lifecycle and is removed on unequip, logout, death, removal, or other explicitly admitted lifecycle event.

The five stages for every commission are meaningful and individually gated:

1. Preparation qualifies the boss core or pattern, role, form, materials, stock, funded quote, and complete immutable blueprint.
2. Forging or weaving constructs the selected weapon, headgear, part, ammunition, or supply form at a qualified city station.
3. Tempering applies the selected primary stat emphasis through the learned engineering specialty and consumed materials.
4. Signature finishing binds the admitted family behavior or support vocabulary with compatible finishing materials.
5. Inspection and attunement validates item form, class/ammo compatibility, exact finished properties, maker identity, and delivery/equip lifecycle before materialization.

## Three best early pilots and counts

The three strongest first pilots are `engineer.field.skylance-repeater`, `engineer.field.aegis-field-carbine`, and `engineer.field.field-repair-coil`: two practical ranged core-gear replacements and one native-route service part. They represent distinct useful baseline forms and keep the initial pilot count at three items rather than expanding a speculative trinket set. A later legendary proof can use `engineer.legendary.onyxia-drakefire-longshot` only after the same form, save/load, mail/trade, equip, effect observation, and clean-removal evidence exists.

Catalogue counts are 18 total: 8 field, 4 masterwork, 6 legendary; 7 ranged-weapon or ranged-weapon-part concepts, 4 headgear concepts, 2 barrier/deployable concepts, 2 controlled-explosion concepts, and 3 repair/support supply concepts. These primary-form counts describe proposals, not native content.

## Native gates before any implementation

- An admitted native item form and client presentation for each weapon, headgear, part, barrier, projector, charge, or supply gadget.
- A real recipe/craft transaction with a city maker and station, qualified profession, specialty, contributor, funded input/payment quote, material reservation, consumption, settlement, and delivery provenance.
- A real ammunition and consumable stock route for every shot, charge, repair input, barrier payload, drone supply, and snare payload.
- Role, class, slot, binding, and equip validation that preserves normal item restrictions and refuses incompatible forms.
- A versioned per-item blueprint and property/effect adapter; no shared-template mutation and no guessed item or spell IDs.
- Native save/load, bank, mail, trade, equip, unequip, logout, death, range, group, and removal lifecycle handling.
- Client-observed stat and effect presentation with exact native qualification of proc trigger, target hostility, range, cooldown, duration, chance, and friendly protection.
- Validation that controlled explosions cannot recursively trigger themselves, damage protected friendlies, force targets, or create invulnerable helper bodies.
- First live proof must cover one admitted ranged or barrier form, funded real inputs and work, actual craft and delivery, save/load/mail/equip, observed bounded behavior, and clean removal before expanding the family.
