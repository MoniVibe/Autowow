# Material shopping lists for the profession catalogue

These are **proposed costs for 90 designs**, not current native recipes. Existing-material names were checked
against the live world item catalogue with read-only metadata queries. Matching an ingredient name does not
admit a recipe, cost, drop or item effect. Custom relic cores below **do not yet exist**.

Field quantities use a **level 68–72 supply reference**. The earlier lower-level descriptions need separate
native tier variants; a novice cannot use these inputs to bypass profession training or wearer-level rules.
Masterwork/legendary quantities are later-progression proposals, not a claim those crafts are available now.

Each row is the complete direct-input bill for its stated output. Bracers/gloves/boots are one equipment item
even when visually paired. Explicit two-item projects say so. Enchants need an existing eligible host item,
gems need real sockets, and a component is not a free new weapon. Fees/payment/postage are quoted separately.

Bolts, heavy leather, bars and engineering components are consumed stock. Their own native child recipes
supply them; do not count both the component and its raw ingredients as two direct costs. Ore prospecting
has no assumed guaranteed gem yield here. Custom boss cores are consumed once per accepted project.

## Where the guild gets the stock

| Stock | Supply route |
|---|---|
| Leather/fur | Actual skinning or purchased/guild stock; heavy leather is a separate qualified craft. |
| Cloth/bolts/special cloth | Actual creature/quest loot, funded trade and city tailoring; no free cloth generator. |
| Bars/bolts/tubes/capacitors | Mining/smelting and engineering child orders, or bought stock. |
| Gems | Actual prospecting/mining/trade; do not promise a fixed number of ore per random gem. |
| Dust/essences/shards/crystals | Authorized spare-item disenchantment or purchases; preserve useful equipment. |
| Elemental reagents/orbs | Actual admitted loot, conversion or trade; availability is a separate gate. |
| Eternium Thread | Existing vendor stock bought with real funds. |
| Custom boss relic core | Proposed earned encounter input. No core item/drop/award is installed. |


## Batches, stages and current limits

For ten ordinary one-item armor pieces, multiply their bill by ten and keep ten native craft/output receipts.
An ammunition row instead specifies 200 rounds in one proposed production lot; confirm actual native output
count before admitting that route. Durable legendary apparatus is replenished with separately stocked payloads;
it does not consume another relic core per shot or regenerate ammunition for free.

The JSON records a proposed stage for every input: construction inputs at 2, tempering at 3, signature/core
at 4 and legendary inspection catalyst at 5. Reserve the quoted bill before work. Current source implements
no stage material adapter; stage completion is not proof an input was consumed.

No price, guild stock availability, native recipe cost or successful craft is inferred from these tables.
First map the desired field item to an actual learned recipe and use its real reagent list. Custom costs
can be tuned after stock/income and real production throughput are observed.
