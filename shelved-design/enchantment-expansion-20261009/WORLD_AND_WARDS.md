# Worldboss enchantments and territorial wards

**Status:** design only; no native item, spell, recipe, hook, drop, or runtime behavior is admitted. The matching machine-readable catalogue is [WORLD_AND_WARDS.json](WORLD_AND_WARDS.json). It contains exactly eight candidates: four legendary enchant commissions and four masterwork territorial wards.

The design reuses historical material names from [MATERIAL_REFERENCE.json](MATERIAL_REFERENCE.json), derived from [MATERIAL_BILLS.json](../profession-catalogues-20261009/MATERIAL_BILLS.json). Existing-name references deliberately carry no native item entry here. The four named relic cores are custom and unimplemented; none is a claimed boss drop. All listed costs, times, charges, and effects are proposals.

## Four legendary enchantments

Each finish replaces one qualified compatible enchant channel on one real buyer-supplied weapon. The host weapon is not included in the material bill. It retains its ordinary base stats. A real city enchanter, weapon maker, core-holder, and any named collaborators have distinct work and custody records. Each relic is consumed once at signature finishing; ordinary-material recharge never asks for another relic.

A legendary artisan needs the custom taught pattern, proposed character level 80 and Enchanting 425 minimum, a real faction-city enchanting station, and continuously observed active work. These gates do not bypass the target server's native restrictions. Each apprenticeship follows an ordinary useful order and a separate owner-accepted commission. One accepted useful finish can earn one custom non-combat maker signature/mastery record per artisan/design; it grants no native skill, XP, reputation, class skill, or boss legacy. Recharging earns no maker mastery. Each legendary item holds 24 charges; this per-item effect limit is not a global limit on hero progression.

Each commission has five active workshop stages: preparation, weaving, tempering, signature finishing, and inspection/attunement. The proposed stage durations total 390 minutes (6 hr 30 min) for Azure Refraction, 390 minutes (6 hr 30 min) for Tainted Volley Counterseal, 400 minutes (6 hr 40 min) for Faultline Conductor, and 390 minutes (6 hr 30 min) for Dreambough Renewal. The relic is consumed only in stage four. Reserved stock, station custody, and stage receipts remain attached to the one commission; time never creates intermediate items.

Charges and cooldowns belong to the exact finished item and persist through equip changes, bank/mail/trade, death, relog, and restart. At zero charges the host keeps only its compatible ordinary baseline. An eligible hostile event consumes at most one charge after durable attribution. PvP, self/friendly/environmental events, periodic damage/healing, duplicate callbacks, and recursive procs are excluded. Recharge consumes only the listed ordinary inputs and restores the listed 24 charges.

## Four masterwork territorial wards

These are installed site fixtures, not wearable enchantments. Enchanters produce them in faction-city stations; an allied engineer or blacksmith separately makes/installs the mount at the owner-authorized site. Ward projects have 50–70 active workshop minutes plus a separate real site-install action. No time, order, capture, or route proposal teleports an artisan or places a fixture.

Physical mode requires an actual visible hostile actor observed by the server within 35 yd and line of sight. The campaign-point and native-stat figures are two alternative adapter versions. Campaign points are not translated into native creature stats, and the versions never stack. The approach ward is alert-only and has no combat-stat version. Unknown actor presence, stale ownership, missing route evidence, or unqualified physical NPC identity leaves an effect held or inactive.

Every installation binds one stable objective/checkpoint ID, current owner/version, authorized custodian, original material lots, charge count, and fixture identity. Its inventory is reserved from the owner's actual available guild stock and consumed once; reserved materials cannot also become troop kits or another order. The ward creates no food or rations, and militia still consumes one ration per living troop at battle entry. It creates no weapons, armor, ammunition, artillery, troops, replacements, healing, resurrection, or free maintenance.

Capture makes a ward dormant and revokes the prior owner's control. It does not seize old guild stock, transfer reservations, move the fixture, or refill charges. The new owner may pay for the next rebind/recharge from its own available stock, using the exact ordinary recharge bill and time above. That service restores only the stated capacity. Unknown custody remains held for reconciliation.

## Historical encounter sources and design boundary

The source encounters are kept separate from the custom relics and proposed effects:

- Blizzard identifies Azuregos as a Blue Dragonflight world boss in Azshara, guarding magical artifacts, with a frosty theme; the AzerothCore script reference lists frost, arcane, and reflection mechanics. Those details inspire the design, not a native enchant. [Blizzard's WoW Classic world-boss notice](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23221132/wow-classic-world-bosses-and-pvp-honor-system-available) · [AzerothCore Azuregos script reference](https://www.azerothcore.org/doxygen/d8/dd0/boss__azuregos_8cpp.html)
- Blizzard's Classic notice places Lord Kazzak at the Tainted Scar in the Blasted Lands and names Shadow Bolt Volley. This catalogue explicitly uses that original encounter identity, not the Outland Doomlord Kazzak. [Blizzard's WoW Classic world-boss notice](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23221132/wow-classic-world-bosses-and-pvp-honor-system-available)
- Blizzard identifies Doomwalker as an Outland world boss and describes the Fel Reaver/Earthquake motif in its anniversary feature. [Burning Crusade Classic Zul'Aman update](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23783585/burning-crusade-classic-zulaman-is-now-open) · [Blizzard 17th anniversary feature](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23745821/celebrate-17-years-of-world-of-warcraft)
- Blizzard lists Ysondre among the four Dragons of Nightmare and separately lists her with original world bosses; Warcraft Wiki is used only for the Classic green-dragon label. [Blizzard's Nightmare Grove notice](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24148556/now-live-temple-ahnqiraj-ruins-of-ahnqiraj-and-nightmare-grove) · [Blizzard's 20th anniversary event](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24146991/go-on-a-shopping-spree-during-the-wow-20th-anniversary-celebration) · [Ysondre Classic reference](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Ysondre_(tactics))

The five-stage timing follows the workshop policy; personal boss receipts, item effects, and maker credit remain separate.
