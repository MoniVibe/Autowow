# A world of legendaries and mythical makers

Approved direction: signature boss relics become useful equipment, crafters earn lasting distinctions for completing
them, and guilds and expedition groups share distinct benefits. This is an expanded design catalogue, not installed
loot, spells, recipes or rewards. The source-only discovery R10 snapshot remains unchanged and unbuilt.

## The connected loop

Earned encounter → actual relic core/pattern → funded city commission → real inputs and qualified artisan →
successful exact-item legendary craft → maker distinction → payment/delivery/equip → wearer and group effects.

Every configured dungeon/raid finale gets a legendary route. Select the penultimate encounter when its theme better
fits the item, and allow explicit complementary routes for memorable encounters. Final encounter mapping must be
qualified from native completion evidence; the examples below do not declare their inspiration bosses to be finales.
Loot rarity, core quantities, material costs and effect amounts remain to balance per tier. Higher difficulties can
supply upgrade inputs for the same design lineage rather than another first-earned mastery award.

City artisans remain city artisans. Legendary commissions give them an earned progression path without sending
them adventuring. Native profession skill/rank/recipe requirements still matter; custom mastery is separate from
native character level, skills and recipes. A knowledge record alone does not teach a missing native recipe.

## Rewards for the artisan

1. **Personal maker mastery.** The first qualifying successful legendary design-family craft gives that artisan one
   permanent, theme-specific mastery record. It opens compatible future finishing techniques or commission
   eligibility, not a blanket bonus to every item. Repeated callbacks, upgrades and mail stages cannot award it again.
2. **Useful commission standing.** Later genuine deliveries can improve custom contribution standing against actual
   demand and fulfilled terms. Listing stock, making unwanted copies, sending the same good through mail or trading
   it between friendly characters does not create another completed commission.
3. **Named distinction.** A maker earns a recognizable epithet and catalogue entry: for example, a future
   “Brokkhelm, Emberforger” or “Magrasha, Winterwright.” These are illustrative names, not existing awards. Public
   records retain maker GUID, design, first craft, specialty and exact output lineage.
4. **Better work and influence.** Notable makers become eligible for funded legendary orders, priority consideration
   in representative resource allocations, workshop leadership and paid expertise. Requisitions still reserve real
   guild/faction stock. Glory, artisan mastery and contribution standing remain separate custom measures.

Competition comes from distinct portfolios, compatible finishes, fulfilled commissions and customer outcomes.
World/faction/guild firsts receive historical recognition; later makers can still earn their own mastery. Recognition
does not give a first finisher a permanent monopoly. Do not treat a raw craft-count leaderboard as proof of quality.

Mastery bonuses belong to the maker; signature effects belong to the exact finished item. Capture skill/specialty
at craft. A later skill gain does not retroactively improve older gear. Compatible finishing profiles have per-item
budgets appropriate to tier and role; no extra uncapped primary-attribute multiplier is implied by legendary quality.
The existing uncapped earned boss-legacy policy is unchanged.

The maker distinction is a functional earned passive, not just a title. For example, Emberforger admits fire-themed
tempering on eligible future armor; Winterwright admits a frost-ward finish; Twinwright admits complementary
martial/caster resonance. Each resulting item is independently crafted, consumes its inputs and records its exact
maker profile. Possessing mastery alone neither creates the item nor bypasses its trained recipe requirements.
Mythical makers become recognizable through combinations of learned specialties and their actual output portfolios.
Their custom standing can grow through useful city work without combat glory or questing.

## Guild-wide and raid-wide distinction

**Guild benefits** come from the guild's captured earned contribution and knowledge, not from borrowing a famous
item for a minute. A qualifying first legendary commission can add a guild workshop chapter, a family of compatible
patterns, a specialty reputation card and eligibility for larger funded regional projects. Permanent guild records
stay with the entitled guild captured at the event; later maker or owner membership changes do not transfer them.

Physical guild relics and banners can be later projects: guild stock supplies them, the guild records the exact
artifact, and a qualified bearer equips or deploys it. Presence effects are local; the knowledge or organizational
benefit can be guild-wide. Buying or borrowing another guild's relic does not fabricate its first-clear history.

**Raid/group benefits** come from actual equipped items or banners and current native group participation.
Examples include resistance auras, a defensive ward pulse, coordinated attack openings and complementary paired
weapons. They follow equipped identity and eligible range/group membership, ending when the source is unequipped,
unavailable or outside admitted conditions. Identical aura-family effects do not multiply just because several
members bring copies; distinct compatible effects remain independent. This is effect attribution, not a cap on
earned legacies or a new legendary loadout limit.

An event can earn personal, guild and faction records when each scope is explicitly entitled. Each record has its
own beneficiary and receipt; “guild-wide” does not silently grant every offline guild member a personal first kill.

These first concepts emphasize weapons and the armor slots the adventurers need: chest, legs, head, shoulders,
bracers, gloves, waist and boots. Trinkets need not dominate the catalogue. Role variants use one admitted family,
not a universal item that gives every class every stat. Existing native iconic legendaries can gain a separately
qualified signature profile; the design must not blindly overwrite their templates or binding rules.

## More ways to make the world distinct

- **Regional relic lines:** copper-era home regions, Outland expeditions and northern campaigns each have recognizable
  material and effect themes. Lower-tier legendaries are valuable at their intended tier.
- **Guild heirlooms:** actual items retain maker and reforger history through native bank/mail/trade paths. Upgrading
  requires a new funded, material-consuming operation and preserves lineage; the original maker distinction is once.
- **Campaign commissions:** a guild can fund an expedition to recover a specific core for a chosen artisan. Adventurer,
  gatherer, crafter and buyer contribution are separately earned and reconciled.
- **Legendary restoration projects:** several real relic fragments and different profession specialists can produce
  one completed item. Count participants' work once, and count the finished good once across handoffs.
- **Maker dynasties and halls:** guild archives display verified makers, notable designs and current available services.
  Prestige can affect order choice and funded access without inventing native faction reputation.
- **Complementary expedition kits:** different legendary designs can work together across roles. Native group limits,
  actual availability and wages still govern mercenary/bodyguard detachments.

Crafted legendary status is a source of goals and character identity. It does not make all ordinary gear obsolete:
ordinary useful crafting supplies progression, repairs and affordable replacements, while relic work is a distinct
funded production chain.

## Persistence and qualification

Use separate immutable records for encounter/loot entitlement, core reservation, funded commission, actual craft,
exact item lineage, maker first-design mastery, useful contribution, guild knowledge and recipient aura application.
Scope, maker, buyer, original guild and native item GUID are captured at the relevant event rather than reconstructed
from later membership or item location. A single five-stone donation and five-stone mail receipt are the same goods
until independently proven otherwise.

Mastery can settle after a verified successful final craft with reconciled inputs/output. Payment/delivery standing
settles only after its own fulfilled operation; an unpaid successful craft is not a paid commission. No rejected
cast, planned order, aggregate cast count, merged-stack event or pre-completion callback fabricates a legendary.

Native item binding, class/level/armor/skill constraints must qualify per route. Maker identity and effect persistence
must survive save/load/equip/trade/mail without duplicating the finished good or proc. Ordinary enchants/sockets and
recipe-provided properties remain intact. Use admitted client-known item/spell data or explicitly scope client work;
a saved custom record alone does not create a visible aura or correct tooltip.

The existing discovery backend remains default-false for transaction admission because the selected core ignores
BEGIN/COMMIT failures and can lose transaction context on statement reconnect. That dependency must be repaired
and tested in isolation before any real legendary or standing settlement. No heavy build is authorized by this
design update; Galaxy work retains resource priority.

First later pilot: one configured dungeon route, one core, one funded city commission, one successful legendary
craft, one once-earned maker distinction, reconciled delivery/equip and one observed item/group effect. Then expand
the catalogue by tier and role. No live source, SQL, config, controls, loot, items, skills, standing or restart changed.
