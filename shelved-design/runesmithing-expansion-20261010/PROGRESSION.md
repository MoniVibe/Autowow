# Runesmithing progression — design only

**Status:** `DESIGN_ONLY_NOT_NATIVE`; `native_active: false`; lane `progression`; 12 complete gear recipes (10 field, 2 masterwork). Every recipe, item component, effect, name-reference bill, station rule, gate, reward and timing here is a proposal. No native Runesmithing skill line, recipe, item entry, client UI, server hook or balance result is claimed.

Runesmithing is a city workshop career that composes one Trigger → Transformation → Release pattern onto a buyer- or guild-supplied, real, eligible piece of equipment. It applies to that existing host, preserves its item GUID, and does not create an output item. It complements ordinary enchanting and gems; it does not replace either. Death-knight class runeforging and its native rules stay intact. Effects are durable, have no charge counter, require no refill or scheduled upkeep, and are keyed to the exact finished item.

## Career and training

The proposed Runesmithing career is a separate custom mastery value from 1 to 450. It grants no character XP, native profession skill, native profession rank, class ability, armor or weapon proficiency, character level, item, reagent or equipment permission. A native UI/skill-line adapter has not been qualified; this remains a design contract rather than a live profession.

|---|---:|---|
| Initial city apprenticeship | 50 | Novice (1–50) |
| Apprentice lesson at 50 | 125 | Apprentice (51–125) |

The listed recipe gate is a minimum custom career value at actual completion. Starting at career 1 makes the first novice field commission usable immediately; the second novice recipe opens at 10. Each one-piece order earns one career point only after a unique successful inscription-application receipt is settled: a player, recipient or guild treasury pays the quoted fee, the complete listed reagent bill is consumed, and an authoritative application receipt records the same buyer- or guild-supplied host GUID with its new inscription revision and exact input consumption. The same host item is returned to a distinct intended recipient character with the inscription applied; no new item or output GUID is created. The recipient character must differ from the maker character but may share the account or guild, including a society bot funded by its guild. If replacing an inscription, the new design must be a genuinely useful functional upgrade for that recipient, with a distinct design ID, new revision, full paid bill and funded demand. One active inscription is allowed per host; the same design ID on that host never earns a second practice point. The receipt binds maker character, order/unit, design, payer/funding receipt, recipient character, host GUID, inscription revision, consumed inputs and application receipt ID. Replayed callbacks, transfers, shipments, refunds, cancellations, reagent shuffles, timer slices, self-recipient applications, duplicate same-design applications, and unneeded or unpaid replacement work cannot earn another point. A batch contains separately funded host applications; each unit has its own application receipt. The career caps at 450. These are custom workshop points only; a rune application grants no native profession skill or character XP.

Paid practice stays useful to adventurers and guild societies: it applies the commissioned equipment service to the recipient's existing compatible host and consumes its full recipe bill. Work happens at the named city station under the workshop custody and stage rules in `WORKSHOPS.md`; active minutes are not career points. In addition to those practice receipts, teaching may be earned through a real funded application: a qualified mentor supervises a learner who has the required custom lesson and career gate, the learner performs the actual paid-input work, and the same host GUID is returned to a distinct intended recipient. The learner receives only normal custom career credit for that application; the mentor receives a non-repeat teaching citation for fame/ledger recognition. No lesson awards native skill or free mastery points.

Custom specialties are credentials, not stat bonuses. `Resonant Arms` requires three distinct settled field applications across at least two weapon patterns, including one hostile-spell pattern; it qualifies a maker for `rune.masterwork.61_70.chromatic_counterpoint`. `Aegis Relief` requires three distinct settled applications across shield and body-armor hosts, including a valid ally-protection or heal pattern; it qualifies a maker for `rune.masterwork.71_80.last-light-lattice`. A qualified teacher may witness the third qualifying job, and the one-time citation is attached to its settlement receipt. Specialty and teaching receipts are bound to distinct work and cannot be replayed, bought, transferred or converted to character power. Maker rewards are workshop fame and teaching eligibility only.

## Runtime bands and gates

The wearer's level selects the recipe band. The host's native required level must be no higher than the wearer level; its native slot, class/subclass, restrictions, existing proficiency, role fit and validated item-power budget must also be compatible with that recipe variant. Item class, subclass, slot, class restriction, native skill/level requirements, and the wearer's existing native proficiency are checked against the actual base item at commission, completion and equip. Shields require native shield proficiency. Plate body armor requires the wearer already to have native plate proficiency. No crafted inscription grants a missing proficiency or bypasses an item requirement. Host equipment is supplied separately by the buyer or guild, is not included in reagent counts, and remains the same real equipment item with one active inscription identity and revision history.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---|

Recipe gates also require a trained custom career cap that permits the listed value, city workshop access, a compatible host, all inputs and a unique funded application order. The masterworks require their named maker specialty. The city artisan does not need a death-knight ability or native runeforging. Ordinary enchanting and gem slots remain independently governed by native rules.

## Complete recipe cards

All effects are conditional and role-shaped, not passive stat-only bonuses. Percentages and caps are proposed tuning values. An item-family proc ICD is stored on the exact item GUID and continues through relog, trade, storage and equipment swaps; duplicate copies of the same design do not multiply one wearer's instance. No charge counter exists and there is no recharge action. Hostile proc sources are NPC-only in this proposal; player/PvP triggers remain excluded pending separate qualification. Genuinely injured allied targets can include players or NPCs. Friendly, self-inflicted, periodic, reflected, environmental, probe, factory, administrative and custom-proc events are excluded. No effect chains, forces movement, resurrects, grants immunity, restores resources, generates equipment or guarantees victory.

### Novice, levels 1–20

**Grounding Reprise** — `rune.field.novice.grounding_reprise` (field). Host: shield with native class/item and shield-proficiency checks enforced. Roles: shield-bearing frontline, protection. Pattern: `guard_block → gather_impact → reprisal_hit`. After the wearer blocks a hostile NPC melee attack, their next direct weapon hit on that attacker within 5 seconds gains 6% weapon-hit damage, capped at 3 damage; 20-second ICD. Career 1. Bill: 1 Copper Rune Blank, 1 Runic Binder, 5 Strange Dust, 1 Lesser Magic Essence. One 12-minute city-work stage.

**Kindled Mending** — `rune.field.novice.kindled_mending` (field). Host: cloth, leather, mail or natively permitted plate body armor. Roles: healer, group support. Pattern: `mercy_heal → weave_life → heal_echo`. When a direct heal restores at least 5% of a genuinely injured ally's maximum health, echo 10% of that heal to the same recipient if still injured, capped at 3; 30-second ICD. Echo cannot trigger itself or another rune. Career 10. Bill: 1 Copper Rune Blank, 2 Runic Binder, 4 Strange Dust, 1 Lesser Magic Essence. One 14-minute stage.

### Apprentice, levels 21–40

**Closing Cadence** — `rune.field.apprentice.closing_cadence` (field). Host: weapon natively usable by the wearer. Roles: melee initiator, pursuit. Pattern: `pursuit_approach → resonate_pair → haste_window`. If the wearer voluntarily moves from at least 12 yards to within 5 yards of an already hostile NPC and lands a direct hostile attack within 3 seconds, grant 4% attack speed for 4 seconds; 30-second ICD. No movement is forced or accelerated. Career 100. Bill: 1 Iron Rune Blank, 1 Runic Binder, 7 Soul Dust, 2 Lesser Mystic Essence, 1 Small Glowing Shard. One 18-minute stage.

**Parry Shelter** — `rune.field.apprentice.parry_shelter` (field). Host: shield, with native class/item, shield-proficiency and parry-eligibility checks enforced. Roles: defender, off-tank. Pattern: `guard_parry → bind_protection → ward_absorb`. After parrying hostile NPC direct melee, absorb the next hostile NPC direct hit within 5 seconds for 2% of wearer maximum health, capped at 16; 30-second ICD. Career 100. Bill: 1 Iron Rune Blank, 2 Runic Binder, 6 Soul Dust, 2 Lesser Mystic Essence, 1 Small Glowing Shard. One 20-minute stage.

### Journeyman, levels 41–60

**Duellist's Spark** — `rune.field.journeyman.duellists_spark` (field). Host: weapon natively usable by the wearer. Roles: battlemage, hybrid damage. Pattern: `cadence_combat → resonate_pair → elemental_burst`. The wearer's successful native melee special hits a hostile NPC, then the wearer lands a hostile NPC direct spell within 6 seconds; the next hostile NPC direct spell within 5 seconds gains 5% spell-power fire damage, capped at 24; 25-second ICD. Career 200. Bill: 1 Mithril Rune Blank, 1 Runic Binder, 9 Vision Dust, 2 Lesser Nether Essence, 1 Small Radiant Shard. One 24-minute stage.

**Held Ground** — `rune.field.journeyman.held_ground` (field). Host: cloth, leather, mail or natively permitted plate body armor. Roles: tank, line-holder. Pattern: `steadfast_hold → bind_protection → escape_window`. While a hostile NPC remains within 8 yards, the wearer must stay within a 1-yard radius of the same position for 5 continuously observed seconds; circling, movement, forced displacement, and position correction reset the hold. The next hostile NPC direct hit grants 20% reduction to root/snare duration applied during the next 4 seconds; 35-second ICD. It does not break existing control or move the wearer. Career 200. Bill: 1 Mithril Rune Blank, 2 Runic Binder, 8 Vision Dust, 2 Lesser Nether Essence, 1 Small Radiant Shard. One 26-minute stage.

### Artisan, levels 61–70

**Measured Onslaught** — `rune.field.artisan.measured_onslaught` (field). Host: weapon natively usable by the wearer. Roles: melee spellblade, fire-combat cadence. Pattern: `cadence_combat → gather_heat → haste_window`. The wearer's successful native melee special hits a hostile NPC, then the wearer lands a successful native Fire spell on a hostile NPC within 8 seconds; this requires the wearer already to have native Fire spell capability. That cadence primes 5% melee haste for 4 seconds; 30-second ICD. No class spell or school proficiency is granted. Career 300. Bill: 1 Adamantite Rune Blank, 1 Runic Binder, 11 Arcane Dust, 3 Lesser Planar Essence, 1 Prismatic Shard. One 30-minute stage.

**Ambush Veil** — `rune.field.artisan.ambush_veil` (field). Host: cloth, leather, mail or natively permitted plate body armor. Roles: scout, backline survival. Pattern: `ambush_survive → bind_protection → ward_absorb`. In a newly identified engagement, when the wearer survives a witnessed hostile NPC opener direct critical hit while at or below 60% health, absorb the next hostile NPC direct spell hit within 5 seconds for 3% of wearer maximum health, capped at 45; 40-second ICD. Only the opener event can prime it; later critical hits in that engagement do not. Career 300. Bill: 1 Adamantite Rune Blank, 2 Runic Binder, 10 Arcane Dust, 3 Lesser Planar Essence, 1 Prismatic Shard. One 32-minute stage.

### Master, levels 71–80

**Bulwark's Answer** — `rune.field.master.bulwarks_answer` (field). Host: weapon natively usable by the wearer. Roles: main tank, counterattack. Pattern: `cadence_combat → gather_impact → reprisal_hit`. While a hostile NPC remains within 8 yards, the wearer first stays within a 1-yard radius of the same position for 8 continuously observed seconds; then takes its hostile direct melee hit and lands a native weapon special against that same attacker within 5 seconds. The counter special gains 7% weapon-hit damage, capped at 70; 35-second ICD. The hold is an eligibility condition; the cadence is the distinct hostile melee hit followed by the successful weapon special. Career 350. Bill: 1 Saronite Rune Blank, 1 Runic Binder, 12 Infinite Dust, 3 Greater Cosmic Essence, 1 Dream Shard. One 34-minute stage.

**Vigilant Benediction** — `rune.field.master.vigilant_benediction` (field). Host: shield with native class/item and shield-proficiency checks, also qualified for the healer-host/armor tags. Roles: healer, protective support. Pattern: `mercy_heal → weave_life → ward_absorb`. While wielding the shield, a direct heal that restores at least 15% of an injured ally's maximum health grants that same ally an absorb for the next hostile NPC direct hit within 5 seconds; 35-second ICD. Absorb is 2% of the wearer's maximum health, capped at 90. Career 350. Bill: 1 Saronite Rune Blank, 2 Runic Binder, 10 Infinite Dust, 3 Greater Cosmic Essence, 1 Dream Shard. One 36-minute stage.

### Masterwork

**Chromatic Counterpoint** — `rune.masterwork.61_70.chromatic_counterpoint` (masterwork). Host: weapon natively usable by the wearer. Roles: battlemage, hybrid damage. Pattern: `spell_alternation → resonate_pair → elemental_burst`. A hostile NPC direct spell of admitted school A, followed by the wearer's distinct native melee special and then a hostile NPC direct spell of a different admitted school B within 12 seconds, primes a final hostile NPC hit within 5 seconds to add 8% of the applicable attack or spell power as arcane damage, capped at 110; 45-second ICD. The final hit can be melee or spell but must target a hostile NPC; this effect cannot prime itself. Career 350 and `Resonant Arms`. Bill: 1 Adamantite Rune Blank, 3 Runic Binder, 18 Arcane Dust, 4 Lesser Planar Essence, 2 Prismatic Shard. Three active stages: Pattern the counterpoint, 18 min; Temper the weapon lattice, 20 min; Inspect event and item identity, 17 min (55 total).

**Last-Light Lattice** — `rune.masterwork.71_80.last_light_lattice` (masterwork). Host: body armor, with native armor proficiency and base-item requirements preserved. Roles: healer, rescue support. Pattern: `mercy_heal → weave_life → heal_echo`. Additional eligibility: while at or below 35% health, the wearer has survived a hostile NPC direct hit within the last 6 seconds. Then a direct heal to a genuinely injured ally echoes 15% of that heal to the same recipient if still injured, capped at 140; 60-second ICD. It cannot echo to full health, revive, chain, or trigger from friendly/self-generated damage. Career 400 and `Aegis Relief`. Bill: 1 Saronite Rune Blank, 3 Runic Binder, 20 Infinite Dust, 5 Greater Cosmic Essence, 2 Dream Shard, 1 Abyss Crystal. Three stages: Prepare the rescue lattice, 20 min; Weave the guarded echo, 25 min; Inspect transfer and no-recursion behavior, 20 min (65 total).

## Component and admission boundary

The six component names are shared proposals: Copper, Iron, Mithril, Adamantite and Saronite Rune Blank, plus Runic Binder. Each recipe consumes only its named blanks/binders and raw reagents; no recipe also charges raw materials for making a blank or binder. The component outputs are `custom_unimplemented`. Raw reagent names are references, not admitted item identities or sourcing claims; every referenced native item entry is null. In particular, the lower-band names require qualification against the target server before any admission. The buyer-supplied host is separate from every input bill.

An eventual admission needs exact item/name qualification, effect-context and durable item-identity hooks, proc/ICD and trade/relog evidence, the custom mastery ledger and trainer/teaching settlement, and an explicitly approved policy for native profession/client presentation and the two-profession limit. Until each is resolved, no production, bot control, native craft, default profession slot displacement, or client skill-line behavior is active. City artisans quote the full bill, fee, station, stages and delivery terms; adventurers, gatherers and guild societies provide earned hosts/reagents and may choose an available artisan. A maker's benefit is distinct paid work, city reputation and teaching credentials, never free item power or character progression.
