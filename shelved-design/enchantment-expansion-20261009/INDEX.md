# Enchantment expansion: schools, legends and wards

Four Luna agents at xhigh produced **32 new proposals**: six field enchants, two masterwork enchants,
twenty raid/world-boss legendary finishes and four territorial wards. They expand the existing profession book.
**Designs only; none is installed in the world yet.** Numbers, costs and drop routes are proposals.

| Source / school | Proposed signature | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Ragnaros — Emberwake | Four weapon hits build heat; the next melee special adds a fire burst. A small fire-resistance passive remains when charges run out. | A recognizable fire weapon for a frontline adventurer. |
| Twin Emperors — Conjoined Discipline | Alternating a melee special and a spell primes one stronger attack of each type. | Rewards hybrid fighting rather than a generic damage buff. |
| Sapphiron — Glacial Watch | A hostile frost hit primes a short personal shield against the next direct hit. | A frost-themed defensive finish usable outside Naxxramas too. |
| Yogg-Saron — Whisper-Cleared Circlet | A deliberate casting pause followed by a hostile spell returns a small, capped amount of mana. | A caster rhythm with a charged, finite benefit. |
| Doomwalker — Faultline Conductor | Taking a melee hit releases a small nearby quake pulse and brief slow. | A defensive melee weapon that feels different from a fire proc. |
| Ysondre — Dreambough Renewal | Healing a genuinely injured ally releases a small nearby healing bloom. | Gives healer weapons a distinctive group role. |
| Last Stand / Oathbound schools | A low-health shield for the wearer, or a brief shield for a threatened ally. | Useful field services and bodyguard equipment. |
| Territorial wards | Local approach warnings, garrison protection, siege disruption and checkpoint shelter. | Repeat commissions for city enchanters and local installers. |

## What a commission costs

**Emberwake example:** one supplied compatible two-handed weapon, **32 Infinite Dust, 8 Greater Cosmic Essence,
4 Dream Shard, 2 Abyss Crystal, 6 Eternal Fire and one proposed Ragnaros Ember Relic**. Five city-work stages take
**300 active minutes**. The pattern entitlement is separate from that consumed relic. The finish starts with
**30 charges**. A **10-minute recharge** uses **5 Infinite Dust, 5 Greater Cosmic Essence and 5 Eternal Fire** to
restore **exactly five missing charges**. It needs no additional boss relic.

Other legendary projects take **240–400 active minutes**, with **24–30 charges**. Each has its own exact bill;
recharge amounts and prices differ and need economy tuning. Field-school cards include five proposed level bands,
appropriate native skill/proficiency gates and separate lower-band reagent bills. Lower-band material-name
references still need qualification. Custom initial school patterns need their own acquisition routes.

City artisans perform the work and earn proposed maker credentials and teaching eligibility. Adventurers supply
earned relics; gatherers and the market supply ordinary reagents. Native character/profession progression remains
separate from custom mastery. Routine stat gear should keep priority over long legendary commissions.
