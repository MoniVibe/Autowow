# Conservative, boss-flavored permanent traits

This replaces the oversized godslayer packages and the generic rating-only rewrite. Three Luna high agents
revised162 proposals across23 raids; root reviewed encounter identity, scale, trigger usefulness and consolidation.
No reward is implemented or active. Each boss teaches a specific small trait; heroes grow through many victories.

## Selected accumulation and raid completion

- One small, encounter-specific permanent trait per first eligible boss-family victory. No generic stat package.
- All earned traits remain active. No loadout limit, daily progression cap or shared legacy power cap.
- Repeated victory, difficulty, size, relog or guild change does not repeat a personal first-kill award.
- Coupled encounters settle once; explicit different optional variants may earn their own individual trait.
- Permanent contribution amounts add within exact effect/condition categories; no recursive multiplication.
- Proc cooldowns regulate individual mechanics only; they do not cap earned progression.
- Native immunities and valid arithmetic remain; no bypass, forced death/revive, resource faucet or guaranteed victory.
- Raid completion groups already-earned traits into a named card. Its summary is never applied in addition to those traits.
- Source receipts remain individually traceable after consolidation. Identical context yields identical power before/after.
- The all-listed summary below includes optional entries; the mandatory completion set needs explicit encounter admission.
- Conditional effects remain conditional in the summary. Rating, resistance, percentage points and seconds remain distinct.
- Faction industrial unlocks remain separate. Artisan workshop progress does not require artisans to join raids.
- This catalogue is an unimplemented proposal; native effects, stacking semantics and PvP policy need qualification.

Amounts are draft values. Rating points are not percentage points or defense/weapon skill. Conditional bonuses
must retain their exact native context, school and attribution. The ledger cannot substitute named fiction for
a supported effect; every modifier needs a tested native adapter before activation.
