# Enchantment book:32 new proposals

Four explicitly selected gpt-6-luna/xhigh design agents produced20 boss-derived legendary recipes, eight school designs and four territorial wards. These expand the preserved profession catalogue; none is a native recipe, item, aura or drop yet.

All costs, proc numbers and timing are proposals. Host gear is supplied separately. Full cards in the four lane documents explain role/rank gates, teaching, maker recognition, provenance and bot service rules. Charges and cooldowns persist across item/owner lifecycle; recharge is an ordinary input-consuming city service. No global boss-legacy progression cap is added.
